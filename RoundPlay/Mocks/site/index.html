01 · START

Course, players, games.

Your regular group is sorted to the top of the roster, one tap each. Pick as many games as you want to run at once and set the stake per game.

02 · SHARE

Three words, said out loud.

Point a camera at the code, or just say otter-maple-jump across the cart. No account, no password, no email. Tap your name and you're in.

03 · SCORE

One screen per hole.

A number strip centred on the hole's par, with five thumb-sized targets that cover almost every real score. It works in a dead zone and reconciles when you're back.

EVERY DOLLAR, EXPLAINED

The scoring argument ends on the 19th hole.

Every entry is attributed and nothing is ever overwritten. A correction shows up as its own line. If someone changed your 6 to a 5 on 14, you can see who and when.

RoundPlay never holds, moves, or takes a cut of money. It keeps the tally; you settle it however you already do.

Six games at launch

Run several at once. Skins plus a Nassau is the normal case. Every group size from two to eight is covered.

Skins

2–8

Every hole is worth a skin. Win the hole outright to take it. Tie and it rolls over.

Nassau

2 OR 4

Three bets in one round: front nine, back nine, and overall. Fall behind and you can press.

Stableford

2–8

Score points per hole instead of counting strokes. A bad hole costs you a point, not your round.

Nines

3 ONLY

Nine points a hole for a threesome: 5 for low, 3 for middle, 1 for high.

Wolf

3–5

Take turns being the Wolf. Pick a partner off the tee, or take on all three alone for quadruple points.

Bingo Bango Bongo

2–4

Three points a hole: first on the green, closest to the pin, first in the hole. Handicap doesn't matter.
